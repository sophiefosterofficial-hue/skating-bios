Bio
Emilea Zingas (born April 22, 2002) and Vadym Kolesnik (born October 27, 2001) are one of the most exciting stories in U.S. figure skating. Zingas competed as a singles skater for Cyprus at the international level before switching to ice dance and teaming up with Kolesnik in March 2022. Kolesnik moved from Ukraine to the United States specifically to train under coach Igor Shpilband in suburban Detroit, and received U.S. citizenship in summer 2025. Zingas plans to pursue sports psychology and volunteers with the National Alliance on Mental Illness.
In just four years together, Zingas and Kolesnik went from complete unknowns to Olympic finalists. They became the only ice dance team making their Olympic debut to finish in the top five at the 2026 Milan Games, finishing fifth — a result that shocked even them. "I would say, 'You're lying,'" Zingas said when told ahead of the Games they might finish there. A month later at the 2026 World Championships in Prague, they went even further, claiming the bronze medal in their first-ever World Championship appearance.
