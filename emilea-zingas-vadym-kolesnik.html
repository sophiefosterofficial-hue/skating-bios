Bio
Emilea Zingas and Vadym Kolesnik are one of the most exciting stories in U.S. figure skating. Zingas competed as a singles skater for Cyprus before switching to ice dance in 2022. Kolesnik moved from Ukraine to the United States to train under Igor Shpilband, and received U.S. citizenship in summer 2025. Zingas plans to pursue sports psychology and volunteers with the National Alliance on Mental Illness.
In just four years together they became Olympic finalists, finishing fifth in Milan — the best result ever for an ice dance team making their Olympic debut. A month later at the 2026 World Championships, they went further still, winning the bronze medal in their first-ever World Championship appearance. "I would say, 'You're lying,'" Zingas said when told before the Games they might finish fifth. Their coach Igor Shpilband called their biggest transformation of the season simply "confidence."
