Vote Now: 2026 SKATING Magazine Readers' Choice of the Year Award

Cast your vote for the Michelle Kwan Trophy — voting closes July 20 at 11:59 p.m. MT
July 2026By: US Figure Skating News
SKATING Magazine
Readers' Choice Award
Vote closes July 20, 2026
U.S. Figure Skating

Each summer, figure skating fans are asked to vote for the eligible skater whom they believe is most deserving of the SKATING magazine Readers' Choice of the Year Award, also known as the Michelle Kwan Trophy, based on their accomplishments of the past season (2025-26).

Eligible skaters for the award are:

  • Top five championship-level skaters in each discipline at the 2026 Prevagen U.S. Figure Skating Championships
  • Medalists at the 2026 World Championships
  • Medalists at the 2026 World Junior Figure Skating Championships
  • Top three senior teams at the 2026 U.S. Synchronized Skating Championships
  • Medalists at the 2026 World Junior Synchronized Skating Championships

The voting window ends July 20 at 11:59 p.m. MT. Fans may vote once during the voting period.
