Bio
Madison Chock (born July 2, 1992, Redondo Beach, California) and Evan Bates (born February 23, 1989, Ann Arbor, Michigan) are the most decorated ice dance team in U.S. figure skating history. They have been partners since 2011, and married in Hawaii in June 2024. They train in Montreal under coaches Marie-France Dubreuil, Romain Haguenauer, and Patrice Lauzon at the IceAcademy of Montreal.
Chock and Bates won three consecutive World Championships from 2023 to 2025, becoming the first U.S. team to accomplish this feat. At the 2026 Milan Olympics, they finally earned the individual Olympic medal that had eluded them, taking silver in ice dance behind France's Fournier Beaudry and Cizeron — combined with their two team event gold medals, they are now tied for the most Olympic medals by a U.S. figure skater. Evan's fifth Olympic appearance made him the first U.S. figure skater to compete in five Olympic Games. Off the ice they have two poodles, Henry and Stella.
