Bio
Madison Chock and Evan Bates are the most decorated ice dance team in U.S. figure skating history. Partners since 2011, they married in Hawaii in June 2024 and train at the IceAcademy of Montreal under coaches Marie-France Dubreuil, Romain Haguenauer, and Patrice Lauzon. They have two poodles, Henry and Stella.
Chock and Bates won three consecutive World Championships from 2023 to 2025, becoming the first U.S. team to accomplish this feat. At the 2026 Milan Olympics they earned their first individual Olympic medal, taking silver in ice dance behind France. Combined with their two team event gold medals, they are tied for the most Olympic medals by any U.S. figure skater. Evan's fifth Olympic appearance made him the first U.S. figure skater to compete in five Olympic Games.
