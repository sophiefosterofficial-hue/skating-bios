Bio
Isabeau Levito (born March 3, 2007) is an American figure skater born in Philadelphia and raised in Mount Holly, New Jersey. She trains under coaches Yulia Kuznetsova, Otar Japaridze, and Slava Kuznetsov, and represents the Skating Club of Southern New Jersey. Her mother is a native of Milan, Italy, and Isabeau speaks both Italian and Russian.
Levito began skating at age three after watching the 2010 Winter Olympics. She won the 2022 World Junior Championships before making a dominant senior debut, winning the US national title at age 15 and earning World silver in 2024. After missing much of the 2024–25 season due to a foot injury, she returned for the Olympic season and finished fourth at the 2026 World Championships in Prague. She won the ISU Best Newcomer award in 2023.
