Bio

Isabeau Levito (born November 9, 2007) is one of the most gifted and versatile ladies skaters in the world. She competes for the Skating Club of New York and is a consistent presence at the top of both the U.S. and international standings.

Levito made an immediate impact upon her arrival on the senior circuit, earning a World Championship silver medal in 2024 and quickly establishing herself as one of the sport's elite competitors. Her skating is defined by an unusually mature sense of interpretation, seamless edge work, and a rare ability to connect music to movement in ways that feel instinctive rather than choreographed. She consistently challenges for Grand Prix medals and has placed in the top ten at every major international assignment she has entered.

At the 2026 Milan Olympics, Levito competed as part of Team USA's formidable women's contingent and followed the Games with a fourth-place finish at the 2026 World Championships in Prague — a performance that underscored her standing as one of the best skaters in the world entering what promises to be an even stronger 2026–27 season.

Levito began skating at age three after watching the 2010 Winter Olympics. She won the 2022 World Junior Championships before making a dominant senior debut, winning the US national title at age 15 and earning World silver in 2024. After missing much of the 2024–25 season due to a foot injury, she returned for the Olympic season and finished fourth at the 2026 World Championships in Prague. She won the ISU Best Newcomer award in 2023.
