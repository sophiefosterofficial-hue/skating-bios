Bio
Isabeau Levito (born March 3, 2007) is an American figure skater born in Philadelphia and raised in Mount Holly, New Jersey. She trains under coaches Yulia Kuznetsova, Otar Japaridze, and Slava Kuznetsov, and represents the Skating Club of Southern New Jersey. Her mother, Chiara Garberi-Levito, is a native of Milan, Italy, and Isabeau has embraced her Italian roots, learning the language alongside Russian, which she picked up through years of training.
Levito began skating at age three after watching the 2010 Winter Olympics. She rose through the junior ranks to win the 2022 World Junior Championships before making a dominant senior debut, winning the US national title at age 15. She earned World silver in 2024, then missed much of the 2024–25 season due to a foot injury. She returned for the Olympic season, placing fourth at the 2026 World Championships in Prague and finishing sixth at the Milan Olympics after a fall in the free skate.
Levito won the ISU Best Newcomer award in 2023. She is known for her fluid skating skills, calm presence under pressure, and ability to make technically demanding programs look effortless.
