Bio
Isabeau Levito (born March 3, 2007) is an American figure skater born in Philadelphia and raised in Mount Holly, New Jersey. She trains under coaches Yulia Kuznetsova, Otar Japaridze, and Slava Kuznetsov, and represents the Skating Club of Southern New Jersey. Her mother, Chiara Garberi-Levito, is a native of Milan, Italy, and Isabeau has embraced her Italian heritage, learning the language alongside Russian, which she picked up through years of training with Russian coaches.
Levito began skating at age three after watching the 2010 Winter Olympics in Vancouver. She rose quickly through the junior ranks, winning the 2022 World Junior Championships before making a dominant senior debut. She won the 2023 US national title at age 15, finished fourth at her first World Championships, and then claimed World silver in 2024. After missing part of the 2024–25 season due to injury, she returned for the 2025–26 Olympic season and earned a spot on the US Olympic team for the Milan Games.
Levito won the ISU Best Newcomer award in 2023. She is known for her fluid skating skills and ability to make technically demanding programs look effortless.
