Bio

Naomi Williams (born July 14, 2006, Seoul, South Korea) and Lachlan Lewer (born January 28, 2003, Brisbane, Australia) are 2024 World Junior bronze medalists and one of the most exciting pairs teams in the U.S. national program. They train under coaches Drew Meekins, Natalia Mishkutenok, and Eddie Shipstad, and compete for the Skating Club of Boston.

Williams and Lewer formed their partnership in 2022, with the Australian-born Lewer making the switch to representing the United States to skate alongside Williams. The combination clicked immediately — within two seasons they had claimed a World Junior bronze medal and established themselves as the most successful junior American pairs team in years. In the 2025–26 season they added a Junior Grand Prix bronze in Latvia before transitioning to senior competition, finishing ninth at the 2026 U.S. Championships. Their long-term ceiling is considered among the highest in the U.S. pairs program.
