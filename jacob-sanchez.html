Bio

Jacob Sanchez is a senior competitor and Grand Prix-assigned member of the U.S. men's team. He competes internationally on the Grand Prix circuit and has established himself as one of the next generation of American men's skaters with a strong 2025–26 season that included a Four Continents berth.

Sanchez brings an athletic and energetic style to the ice, with programs that combine technical content with performance quality. His continued presence at the top level of international competition in only his second senior season signals a bright trajectory for one of the more exciting young men in the U.S. program.
