Bio
Starr Andrews (born June 23, 2001) is an American figure skater from Los Angeles, California. She trains at LA Elite Ice in Lakewood under coaches Derrick Delmore, Johnathan Cassar, and Anastasia Sharenkova, and represents the Los Angeles Figure Skating Club.
Andrews became an internet sensation at age nine when a video of her skating to Willow Smith's 'Whip My Hair' garnered over 57 million views. She made history as the first Black American woman to win a Grand Prix medal in singles and the first Black woman to medal at the US national championships since Debi Thomas in 1988. In her final season as a singles skater, she performs her own singing voice in the free skate — a remarkable artistic statement to close her singles career.
