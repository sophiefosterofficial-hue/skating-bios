Bio
Starr Andrews (born June 23, 2001) is an American figure skater from Los Angeles, California. She trains under coaches Derrick Delmore, Johnathan Cassar, and Anastasia Sharenkova and represents the Los Angeles Figure Skating Club. Andrews was home-schooled throughout her competitive career and grew up alongside her siblings Skylar, a gymnast, and Ashton, a baseball player.
Andrews made her mark in figure skating history as the first Black American woman to win a Grand Prix medal in the singles discipline, taking silver at the 2022 Skate Canada International. Known for her expressive skating style and dynamic presentation, she has finished in the top ten at three ISU Championships and consistently represents the United States at the senior international level. Her performances are known for their musicality and connection to her programs.
Career Accomplishments
  • 2022 Skate Canada International silver medalist
  • First Black American woman to win a Grand Prix medal in singles
  • 2023 US Championships pewter medalist
  • 2023 CS Golden Spin of Zagreb bronze medalist
  • Top-10 finish at three ISU Championships
  • 2017 US junior silver medalist
