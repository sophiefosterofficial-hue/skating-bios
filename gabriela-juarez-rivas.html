Bio
Gabriela Juarez-Rivas (born May 5, 2008) is an American figure skater competing in ladies singles. A native of San Jose, California, she trains at Sharks Ice San Jose under coaches Olga Ganicheva, Aleksey Letov, and Rafael Arutyunyan, and works with choreographer Shae-Lynn Bourne. She represents the Peninsula Skating Club.
Juarez-Rivas rose quickly through the junior ranks, claiming the World Junior Championship and Junior Grand Prix Final in the 2023–24 season. She transitioned to the senior level for 2024–25, finishing fourth at US Nationals and second at the Grand Prix Final in her debut year. After a difficult 2025–26 Olympic season, she delivered the performance of her career at the 2026 World Championships in Prague, skating her Carmen free skate to claim the bronze medal and her first senior World Championship podium finish.
