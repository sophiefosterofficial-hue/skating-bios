Bio
Gabriela Juarez-Rivas (born May 5, 2008) is an American figure skater competing in ladies singles. A native of Mountain View, California, she trains at Sharks Ice San Jose under coaches Olga Ganicheva, Aleksey Letov, and Rafael Arutyunyan, and works with choreographer Shae-Lynn Bourne. She represents the Peninsula Skating Club.
Juarez-Rivas burst onto the international scene as a junior, claiming the World Junior Championship title in 2024 and the Junior Grand Prix Final in the same season. She transitioned to the senior level for the 2024–25 season, winning both the Grand Prix Final and the US Championships in her senior debut year. After a more difficult 2025–26 Olympic season, she delivered the performance of her career at the 2026 World Championships in Prague, skating her Carmen free skate to claim the bronze medal and her first senior World Championship podium finish.
