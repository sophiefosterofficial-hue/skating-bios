About the ASUPP Fund

The U.S. Figure Skating Athlete Support (ASUPP) Fund provides financial assistance to athletes named to Team Envelopes A, B, C and D to help offset training and competition expenses.

Team envelope assignments are based on results from the prior season. Athletes qualifying for more than one tier are always placed in the higher tier. Stipend amounts are determined annually by U.S. Figure Skating and are not publicly disclosed.

Team A Criteria
A1
1st–3rd at the ISU World Figure Skating Championships or Olympic Winter Games (excluding team event). Top 1–6 in ISU season's best scores or world standings (senior).
A2
4th–10th at the ISU World Figure Skating Championships or Olympic Winter Games. Top 7–12 in ISU season's best scores or world standings (senior).
Team B Criteria
B1
11th or lower at the ISU World Figure Skating Championships or Olympic Games. 2nd–4th at the U.S. Figure Skating Championships. Top 13–24 in ISU season's best scores or world standings (senior).
B2
5th–6th at the U.S. Figure Skating Championships. Top 1–24 in ISU season's best scores or world standings (junior). 2nd–3rd at ISU Junior Grand Prix Final.
Team C & D Criteria
C1
7th–8th at the U.S. Figure Skating Championships. Team USA 2nd–3rd alternates for the Olympics or Worlds. 4th–10th at Four Continents.
C2
Junior athletes placing lower at the U.S. Championships where the event was strong. Athletes meeting criteria for B or C who did not qualify for the U.S. Championships.
D
Athletes assigned to an international competition who do not meet Team A, B or C criteria. Reserve athletes receive no funding.
