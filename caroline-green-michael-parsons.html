Bio

Caroline Green and Michael Parsons are 2026 Four Continents silver medalists and one of the most consistently strong ice dance teams in the United States. Coaches Patrice Lauzon, Marie-France Dubreuil, and Romain Haguenauer — the same legendary team that guides Madison Chock and Evan Bates — have helped shape them into international-caliber performers.

Green and Parsons have built a reputation for technical precision and the kind of rhythmic ice coverage that defines elite ice dance. Their Four Continents silver in 2026 was the best result of their partnership and announced them as genuine contenders at the global level. Finishing fourth at the U.S. Championships in a field that included three Olympic-level teams speaks to the depth of their ability.
