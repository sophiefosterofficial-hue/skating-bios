Bio
Amber Glenn (born October 28, 1999) is an American figure skater and three-time US national champion. A native of Plano, Texas, she trains at the US Olympic and Paralympic Training Center in Colorado Springs under coaches Damon Allen and Tammy Gambill. She represents the Dallas Figure Skating Club.
Glenn is the first American woman to win three consecutive US national titles since Michelle Kwan (2003–05). She is also the first openly LGBTQ+ woman to represent the United States in Olympic singles figure skating, having come out as bisexual and pansexual in 2019. At the 2026 Winter Olympics in Milan, she won gold in the team event and finished fifth in the individual event, becoming one of the oldest US women to compete in Olympic singles figure skating since 1928.
Known for her powerful skating style and ability to land the triple Axel, Glenn is also a passionate mental health advocate through her initiative "Believe and Breathe."
Career Accomplishments
  • 2026 Olympic team event gold medalist
  • 2026 Olympian (5th, individual event)
  • Three-time US champion (2024, 2025, 2026)
  • 2024–25 Grand Prix Final champion
  • Six-time ISU Grand Prix medalist
  • Five-time ISU Challenger Series medalist
  • First openly LGBTQ+ US women's singles Olympian
