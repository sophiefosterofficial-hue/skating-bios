Bio
Andrew Torgashev (born May 29, 2001) is an American figure skater from Coral Springs, Florida. He trains under coaches Rafael Arutyunyan and Brandon Frazier at Lake Arrowhead, California, and represents the Panthers Figure Skating Club. His parents both toured with ice shows before settling in the United States, making him part of the rich tradition of second-generation skaters on the 2026 U.S. Olympic team.
Torgashev is known as an all-or-nothing competitor who pushes his technical content to the limit. He claimed back-to-back U.S. silver medals in 2025 and 2026, and represented the United States at the 2026 Milan Olympics — his first Olympic Games.
