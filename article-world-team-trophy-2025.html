Team USA Defends World Team Trophy Gold in Tokyo with Three World Champions Leading the Way

April 2025

TOKYO — Riding the momentum of its historic three-gold performance at the World Championships in Boston, Team USA defended its World Team Trophy title in Tokyo, led by three World champions competing together for the first time since their individual victories weeks earlier.

Alysa Liu, Ilia Malinin, and Madison Chock and Evan Bates headlined the American squad alongside team captain Jason Brown, Amber Glenn, and Ellie Kam and Danny O'Shea. It marked the first time all three of that year's World champions competed on the same team at the event.

"We've got something special right now," Brown said ahead of the competition. "When you see what this team did in Boston, and then you come here together — there's a real sense of pride in wearing Team USA."

Chock and Bates, competing in their fifth World Team Trophy, opened the event in dominant fashion. In 2023, the couple set world records in the rhythm dance, free dance, and total score at the event, and they arrived in Tokyo having just claimed their third consecutive World title.

Liu's free skate drew the largest crowd reaction of the competition — less than two weeks removed from her stunning World Championship upset, the 19-year-old appeared to have shed any lingering nerves and skated with the freedom of someone who had already accomplished everything she set out to do.

Malinin, undefeated in individual competition since December 2023, treated the Tokyo crowd to another high-wire free skate. Six quadruple jumps, including the quadruple Axel that has become his calling card, left the crowd on its feet. Glenn contributed a strong free skate performance and earned the free skate gold in the ladies event.

Team USA won the overall competition, claiming its second consecutive World Team Trophy title and extending its standing as the world's dominant figure skating nation heading into the Olympic year.
