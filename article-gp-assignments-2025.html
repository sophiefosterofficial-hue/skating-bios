Team USA's 2025 Grand Prix Assignments Announced

Malinin headlines France opener; Broussard, Shin/Nagy, and Wolfkostin/Tsarevski make Grand Prix debuts
6/6/2025By: U.S. Figure Skating
Add photo
U.S. Figure Skating

U.S. Figure Skating has announced Team USA's assignments for the 2025–26 ISU Grand Prix of Figure Skating series, which opens with the Grand Prix de France in Angers, France, on October 17–19.

Ilia Malinin, the reigning World champion and two-time Grand Prix Final champion, leads the men's field at the Grand Prix de France alongside teammates Maxim Naumov and Andrew Torgashev. In the ladies event, Isabeau Levito and Elyce Lin-Gracey will represent the United States — Lin-Gracey making her senior Grand Prix debut.

Alysa Liu and Amber Glenn headline Cup of China, while Sarah Everhardt, Bradie Tennell, and Isabeau Levito travel to Skate Canada International in Saskatoon alongside Malinin and Grand Prix debutant Lucas Broussard. Broussard, the 2024 CS Cranberry Cup champion, earned his first senior Grand Prix assignment off the strength of a standout 2024–25 season.

Skate America in Lake Placid features Liu as the women's headliner alongside Jason Brown in the men's event. Finlandia Trophy includes Glenn, Gabriela Juarez-Rivas, and Tennell in the ladies field, with Brown and Broussard also assigned.

Several teams and skaters will make their Grand Prix Series debut this season, marked below with an asterisk.

Full 2025 Grand Prix Assignments

GP Grand Prix de France (Angers, France — Oct. 17–19, 2025)
Ladies: Isabeau Levito, Elyce Lin-Gracey | Men: Ilia Malinin, Maxim Naumov, Andrew Torgashev | Pairs: Audrey Shin & Balázs Nagy* | Ice Dance: Emily Bratti & Ian Somerville, Eva Pate & Logan Bye

GP Cup of China (Chongqing, China — Oct. 24–26, 2025)
Ladies: Amber Glenn, Alysa Liu | Men: Camden Pulkinen, Jacob Sanchez* | Pairs: Katie McBeath & Daniil Parkman | Ice Dance: Madison Chock & Evan Bates, Caroline Green & Michael Parsons, Emilea Zingas & Vadym Kolesnik

GP Skate Canada International (Saskatoon, Canada — Oct. 31–Nov. 2, 2025)
Ladies: Sarah Everhardt, Isabeau Levito, Bradie Tennell | Men: Lucas Broussard*, Ilia Malinin | Pairs: Ellie Kam & Danny O'Shea | Ice Dance: Emily Bratti & Ian Somerville, Christina Carreira & Anthony Ponomarenko, Leah Neset & Artem Markelov

GP NHK Trophy (Osaka, Japan — Nov. 7–9, 2025)
Ladies: Sarah Everhardt, Elyce Lin-Gracey | Men: Jimmy Ma, Andrew Torgashev | Pairs: Emily Chan & Spencer Howe, Alisa Efimova & Misha Mitrofanov | Ice Dance: Caroline Green & Michael Parsons, Maia Shibutani & Alex Shibutani, Katarina Wolfkostin & Dimitry Tsarevski*

GP Skate America (Lake Placid, New York — Nov. 14–16, 2025)
Ladies: Alysa Liu | Men: Jason Brown | Pairs: Olivia Flores & Luke Wang*, Valentina Plazas & Maximiliano Fernandez | Ice Dance: Christina Carreira & Anthony Ponomarenko, Madison Chock & Evan Bates

GP Finlandia Trophy (Helsinki, Finland — Nov. 21–23, 2025)
Ladies: Amber Glenn, Gabriela Juarez-Rivas, Bradie Tennell | Men: Lucas Broussard, Jason Brown, Jimmy Ma | Pairs: Alisa Efimova & Misha Mitrofanov, Ellie Kam & Danny O'Shea | Ice Dance: Oona Brown & Gage Brown, Maia Shibutani & Alex Shibutani, Emilea Zingas & Vadym Kolesnik

GP Rostelecom Cup (Sochi, Russia — Nov. 28–30, 2025)
Ladies: Gabriela Juarez-Rivas | Ice Dance: Amy Cui & Jonathan Rogers

* denotes Grand Prix Series debut. Lineups subject to change.
