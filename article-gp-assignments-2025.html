Team USA's 2025 Grand Prix Assignments Announced

June 6, 2025

U.S. Figure Skating has announced Team USA's assignments for the 2025–26 ISU Grand Prix of Figure Skating series, which opens with the Grand Prix de France in Angers, France, on October 17–19.

Ilia Malinin, the reigning World champion and two-time Grand Prix Final champion, leads the men's field at the Grand Prix de France alongside teammates Maxim Naumov and Andrew Torgashev. Malinin, who has not lost an individual competition since December 2023, will look to extend that streak as the season opener kicks off what promises to be a historic Olympic season.

In the ladies event at the France competition, Isabeau Levito and Elyce Lin-Gracey will represent the United States. Lin-Gracey, who won the World Junior bronze medal last spring, will make her senior Grand Prix debut in Angers — one of several exciting milestones for Team USA's next generation this fall.

Alysa Liu and Amber Glenn will headline the ladies event at Cup of China, while Sarah Everhardt and Bradie Tennell will travel to Skate Canada International in Saskatoon alongside men's skaters Malinin and Lucas Broussard. Broussard, who won the 2024 CS Cranberry Cup and earned a spot at the 2022–23 Junior Grand Prix Final, makes his senior Grand Prix debut at Skate Canada.

Skate America in Lake Placid, New York, features Liu as the women's headliner, with Jason Brown among the men's entries. Brown, a two-time Olympian and perennial crowd favorite, will compete on home soil in New York.

Multiple U.S. pairs and ice dance teams will make their Grand Prix Series debuts this season, including Audrey Shin and Balázs Nagy in pairs and Katarina Wolfkostin and Dimitry Tsarevski in ice dance, both marked as first-time Grand Prix competitors.

Full 2025 Grand Prix Assignments

Grand Prix de France (Angers, France — Oct. 17–19): Ladies: Levito, Lin-Gracey | Men: Malinin, Naumov, Torgashev | Pairs: Shin/Nagy* | Ice Dance: Bratti/Somerville, Pate/Bye

Cup of China (Chongqing, China — Oct. 24–26): Ladies: Glenn, Liu | Men: Pulkinen, Sanchez* | Pairs: McBeath/Parkman | Ice Dance: Chock/Bates, Green/Parsons, Zingas/Kolesnik

Skate Canada International (Saskatoon, Canada — Oct. 31–Nov. 2): Ladies: Everhardt, Levito, Tennell | Men: Broussard, Malinin | Pairs: Kam/O'Shea | Ice Dance: Bratti/Somerville, Carreira/Ponomarenko, Neset/Markelov

NHK Trophy (Osaka, Japan — Nov. 7–9): Ladies: Everhardt, Lin-Gracey | Men: Ma, Torgashev | Pairs: Chan/Howe, Efimova/Mitrofanov | Ice Dance: Green/Parsons, Shibutani/Shibutani, Wolfkostin/Tsarevski*

Skate America (Lake Placid, New York — Nov. 14–16): Ladies: Liu | Men: Brown | Pairs: Flores/Wang*, Plazas/Fernandez | Ice Dance: Carreira/Ponomarenko, Chock/Bates

Finlandia Trophy (Helsinki, Finland — Nov. 21–23): Ladies: Glenn, Juarez-Rivas, Tennell | Men: Broussard, Brown, Ma | Pairs: Efimova/Mitrofanov, Kam/O'Shea | Ice Dance: O.Brown/G.Brown, Shibutani/Shibutani, Zingas/Kolesnik

* denotes Grand Prix Series debut
