Bio
Ellie Kam and Danny O'Shea have been partners since September 2022 and train in Colorado Springs under coach Drew Meekins. Their nearly 14-year age gap is the largest of any U.S. pairs team to compete at Worlds since at least 1990. Kam began skating at age four after watching her brothers, and previously skated with Ian Meyh before teaming with O'Shea.
In just three seasons together they won the U.S. title in 2024 and earned a spot on the 2026 Olympic team. At the Milan Games they contributed to Team USA's gold in the team event and finished ninth in the individual pairs event. They are known for their spectacular lifts — O'Shea has described them as "some of the best in the entire world." Following the Olympics, Kam and O'Shea withdrew from the 2026 World Championships due to injury.
