Team USA's 2026-27 Grand Prix Assignments Announced

37 U.S. athletes set across six events; Liu, Glenn, and Malinin headline Skate America in Everett
6/16/2026By: U.S. Figure Skating
Add photo
Getty Images

The International Skating Union has announced the athlete assignments for the 2026-27 ISU Grand Prix of Figure Skating Series, with 37 U.S. athletes set to compete across the six-event circuit. The series opens October 23 in Angers, France, and concludes November 29 in Tokyo, with the Grand Prix Final to follow December 10-13 in Chongqing, China.

Olympic and World champion Alysa Liu headlines Skate America alongside fellow Blade Angel Amber Glenn, who makes her sixth appearance at the U.S. stop. Four-time national champion Ilia Malinin and Jason Brown join them in Everett before both men travel to Tokyo for the season finale at NHK Trophy. Full assignments for all U.S. athletes are listed below.
