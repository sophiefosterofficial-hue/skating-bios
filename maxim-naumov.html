Bio
Maxim Naumov (born August 1, 2001) is an American figure skater from Simsbury, Connecticut. He trains at the Skating Club of Boston under coaches Vladimir Petrenko and Benoît Richaud. His parents, Vadim Naumov and Evgenia Shishkova — a pairs World champion duo who competed for Russia at two Olympics — were killed when their American Airlines flight collided with a military helicopter near Washington D.C. in January 2025.
Naumov had spoken with his parents every day about his dream of making the Olympic team. Less than a year after their deaths, he earned his spot on the 2026 U.S. Olympic team with a bronze medal performance at U.S. Nationals in St. Louis, receiving his Team USA jacket in an emotional moment that moved the arena to tears. He now also serves as director of the youth skating program his parents created, honoring their legacy while competing at the highest level.
