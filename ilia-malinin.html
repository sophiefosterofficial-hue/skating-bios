Bio
Ilia Malinin (born December 2, 2004) is an American figure skater and two-time World champion known as the "Quad God." Born in Fairfax, Virginia, and raised in Vienna, he trains under his parents — Tatiana Malinina and Roman Skorniakov — both decorated Olympians who represented Uzbekistan, alongside Rafael Arutyunyan. He represents the Washington Figure Skating Club.
Malinin became the first skater in history to land a quadruple Axel in competition in September 2022, and holds the world record total score of 333.81. He won back-to-back World Championships in 2024 and 2025, and entered the 2026 Milan Olympics as the overwhelming favorite for gold. However, two falls in the free skate dropped him to eighth place in one of the sport's most shocking upsets. He rebounded at the 2026 World Championships in Prague, posting a personal-best 111.29 in the short program.
