Olympic Champions Headline 2026 Stifel Stars on Ice Tour, Visiting 28 Cities Across the U.S.

Alysa Liu, Ilia Malinin, Madison Chock and Evan Bates lead a star-studded cast fresh from the Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic Winter Games, with guest skaters announced for select performances.
March 5, 2026 Tour
Add photo
The 2026 Stifel Stars on Ice tour kicks off April 16 at Hertz Arena in Fort Myers, Florida, and concludes May 31 in Southaven, Mississippi.

U.S. Figure Skating returns from Milan with one of the most decorated American teams in Winter Olympic history. The 2026 Stifel Stars on Ice tour will bring the opportunity to celebrate their achievements to 28 cities across the country, kicking off April 16 in Fort Myers, FL and concluding May 31 in Southaven, MS.

Headlining the tour is Olympic Champion and Olympic Team Gold Medalist, reigning World Champion and two-time U.S. Champion Alysa Liu, whose historic comeback culminated in the first U.S. women's Olympic Gold since 2002. She is joined by Olympic Team Gold and Ice Dance Silver Medalists, three-time reigning World Champions and seven-time reigning U.S. Champions Madison Chock and Evan Bates; and Olympic Team Gold Medalist, reigning three-time World Champion and reigning four-time U.S. Champion Ilia Malinin, the "Quad God," who anchored the U.S. Team's second consecutive Olympic Gold.

Additional cast members include Olympic Team Gold Medalist and reigning three-time U.S. Champion Amber Glenn; Olympic Team Gold Medalists and U.S. Champions Ellie Kam and Danny O'Shea; 2026 Olympic Team member, World Silver Medalist and U.S. Champion Isabeau Levito; Olympic Team Bronze Medalist and U.S. Champion Jason Brown; 2026 Olympian Andrew Torgashev; and 2026 Olympians Emily Chan and Spencer Howe.

"This spring's Stars on Ice tour will bring together the very best in the world for an unforgettable, high-energy show filled with artistry, athleticism, and pure magic."

Guest Skaters

Stifel Stars on Ice has also announced an exciting lineup of special guest skaters for its United States tour. 2026 U.S. Olympic Ice Dancers Christina Carreira and Anthony Ponomarenko are scheduled to appear in 19 performances across Newark, Hershey, Long Island, Rochester, St. Louis, Chicago, St. Paul, Loveland, West Valley City, Anaheim, San Jose, Spokane, Wenatchee, Seattle, Portland, Tempe, Allen (both shows) and Southaven. 2026 World Medalists and U.S. Olympic Ice Dancers Emilea Zingas and Vadym Kolesnik will join the tour for 10 shows. 2026 U.S. pewter medalist Jacob Sanchez will be a guest star in the Springfield, MA performance, and Team USA competitor Liam Kapeikis will be a guest star in Wenatchee, WA.

Additionally, 2026 World bronze medalist and 2024 World Junior Champion Gabriela Juarez-Rivas will join the tour as a guest performer in Anaheim (May 16), San Jose (May 17), Tempe (May 27), Allen, TX (May 29 and 30), and Southaven, MS (May 31).

For a complete schedule detailing which cities will feature each guest appearance, visit starsonice.com.
