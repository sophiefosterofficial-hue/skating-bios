Bio
Emily Chan and Spencer Akira Howe are an American pairs team based in Colorado Springs. Spencer enlisted in the U.S. Army's World Class Athlete Program in October 2024 and attended basic training at Fort Carson before returning to full-time skating. His goal is to eventually become an Army chaplain. "The Army is definitely a bigger commitment, but at the same time, my duty right now is skating," Howe said at the Games.
At the 2026 Milan Olympics, Chan and Howe delivered their finest free skate of the season, climbing from ninth after the short program to finish seventh overall. They were subsequently called up to compete at the 2026 World Championships in Prague after Kam and O'Shea withdrew due to injury — a remarkable opportunity in their first Olympic season.
