Bio

Olivia Flores and Luke Wang are a Colorado Springs-based senior pairs team who train under coaches Natalia Mishkutenok and Eddie Shipstad at the Thunderbirds Figure Skating Club, with choreography by Drew Meekins. Olivia (born August 12, 2007) is the younger sister of ice dancer Isabella Flores, making the Flores family one of the most remarkable in U.S. figure skating.

Flores and Wang are among the most promising development pairs in the country, training in one of the sport's most elite environments. Their steady growth through the national ranks and technical work under Mishkutenok — a World champion in her own right — positions them well for greater international exposure in the seasons ahead.
