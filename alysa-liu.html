Bio
Alysa Liu (born August 8, 2005) is an American figure skater and the 2026 Olympic champion in both women's singles and the team event. Born in Clovis and raised in Oakland, California, she trains under coaches Phillip DiGuglielmo and Massimo Scali and represents the St. Moritz Skating Club. She is studying psychology at UCLA and is the oldest of five siblings.
Liu first became a sensation at age 13, winning the US national championship and becoming the first female skater to land both a triple Axel and a quadruple jump in one program. After a two-year hiatus — during which she hiked to the Everest Base Camp — she made a remarkable comeback, winning the 2025 World Championship in Boston, the first US women's world title in 19 years. She completed her story at the 2026 Milan Olympics with gold in both the individual and team events, becoming the first US woman to win Olympic gold in women's singles since Sarah Hughes in 2002.
