Bio
Christina Carreira and Anthony Ponomarenko have been partners since April 2014 and train at the Ice Academy of Montreal's Ontario campus under coaches Scott Moir, Madison Hubbell, Adrián Díaz, and Patrice Lauzon. Christina was born in Montreal with two dislocated hips and was told she would never run or play sports. French is her first language. She obtained U.S. citizenship in November 2025 after a seven-year journey that required years of making the hours-long drive from Michigan to London, Ontario, five days a week.
Anthony is the son of Marina Klimova and Sergei Ponomarenko — the only ice dance team in history to win an Olympic medal of every color (bronze 1984, silver 1988, gold 1992). It was Anthony's mother who spotted Christina at a competition in Lake Placid and suggested the pairing. Their coach Scott Moir is himself a two-time Olympic champion with Tessa Virtue. Anthony underwent ankle surgery in 2022 to address damage from a 2015 sprain, and the team has steadily climbed the senior ranks since, earning their first Olympic berth in 2026.
