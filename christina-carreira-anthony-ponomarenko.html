Bio
Christina Carreira (born April 3, 2000, Montreal) and Anthony Ponomarenko (born January 5, 2001, San Jose) have been partners since April 2014 and train at the Ice Academy of Montreal's Ontario campus under coaches Scott Moir, Madison Hubbell, Adrián Díaz, and Patrice Lauzon. Christina was born in Montreal with two dislocated hips and was told she would never run or play sports — yet went on to become an Olympian. French is her first language. She obtained U.S. citizenship in November 2025, just in time to compete at the Milan Games.
Anthony is the son of Marina Klimova and Sergei Ponomarenko — the only ice dance team in history to win an Olympic medal of every color (bronze 1984, silver 1988, gold 1992 for the Soviet Union). It was Anthony's mother who spotted Christina at a competition in Lake Placid and suggested the pairing. Their coach Scott Moir is himself a two-time Olympic champion. Following an ankle surgery in 2022 to address damage from a 2015 sprain, Anthony returned stronger and the team has steadily climbed the senior ranks, earning their first Olympic berth in 2026.
